Personal air quality for urban movers.

AirTrack delivers personalised, forward-looking air quality guidance for runners, cyclists, and commuters: the best time to go, the cleanest route, and what you actually breathed.

The problem

Air quality data exists. Personal exposure data doesn't.

Air quality is measured at a national and regional level. But personal exposure, specific to the road you run on at the time you run, does not exist as actionable data.

People who care about their health have no tool to act on that concern in the context of their daily movement. AirTrack closes that gap.

EQ's role

We led the full product lifecycle.

From qualitative user research with six participants across interviews and focus groups, that produced the core "Aware Mover" persona, through CX design, product strategy, and engineering coordination with the Arcanys development team.

We also designed the growth model: Strava integration as the primary acquisition channel, a freemium conversion architecture, and an event-led marketing strategy anchored by a London Marathon 2026 partnership.

Built in-house: a proprietary hyperlocal air quality model (V2) incorporating LiDAR, building-height data, and open-source traffic and terrain layers, reducing dependency on third-party APIs.
