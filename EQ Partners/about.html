About

Built by a builder.

EQ Partners was founded by Dan Harris — a strategy consultant and product builder who has spent his career at the intersection of commercial strategy and product development.

The studio model reflects a simple belief: the best products are built by people who care about both the problem and the outcome. Not agencies optimising for billable hours. Not dev shops executing a spec. Partners who are invested in whether it works.

EQ collaborates with a network of specialists — designers, engineers, researchers, growth operators — assembled around each venture. The result is a studio that can move like a startup and think like a consultancy.
