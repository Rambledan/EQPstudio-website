## 2. About EQ Partners

EQ Partners is a product development studio that collaborates with founders and organisations to take product ideas from concept to market. The model is partnership-first: EQ embeds with a partner to co-build ventures, providing full-stack capability across every stage of the product lifecycle — from initial strategy and customer insight through to engineering, go-to-market, and customer success.

EQ is not a traditional agency. It doesn't take briefs and deliver outputs. It takes a stake in the problem and builds alongside the people who own it.

**Core differentiators:**
- Full-stack method spanning strategy, CX design, marketing, sales, engineering, and customer success — all under one roof
- AI-augmented workflows built into how the studio operates (not bolted on)
- A track record of shipping real products across consumer apps, B2B platforms, and internal tooling
- A proprietary Product OS — an agentic product intelligence layer built in-house

### 7.3 Case Study: Borrow It

**Slug:** `/work/borrow-it`

**Overview**
Borrow It is a peer-to-peer borrowing and lending platform that lets people access items they need without buying them — and earn from things they own but rarely use. Built on a sharing economy model, it includes a rental comparison tool that aggregates listings from businesses, individual owners, and existing rental platforms into a single discovery layer.

**The Problem**
Ownership is expensive, wasteful, and often unnecessary. Most households contain dozens of items used once a year — drills, camping gear, specialist tools. Meanwhile, those who can't afford to buy or who lack the space to store want access without the cost. No single platform had aggregated this demand cleanly.

**EQ's Role**
EQ Partners led product strategy and experience design — defining the core user journeys for borrower and lender, the trust and safety model (anonymous communication pre-agreement, then contact exchange), and the product architecture. The studio also scoped the Rental Comparison Tool as a key differentiator: a meta-search layer that surfaces options from multiple sources, giving users genuine choice.

**Disciplines applied:** Product Strategy · UX/CX Design · Proposition Development · Go-to-Market

**Outcomes / Status**
- Live product at brw.it
- Platform supports both P2P and B2C rental discovery
- Sustainability positioning: enables access without ownership, reducing consumption and idle assets

### 7.4 Case Study: AirTrack

**Slug:** `/work/airtrack`

**Overview**
AirTrack is a personal air quality app for urban movers — runners, cyclists, and commuters who want to understand and reduce their exposure to pollution. The app delivers personalised, forward-looking air quality guidance: the best time to go, the cleanest route, and what you actually breathed.

**The Problem**
Air quality data exists at a national and regional level. Personal exposure data — specific to the road you run on, at the time you run — does not. People who care about their health have no tool to act on that concern in the context of their daily movement. AirTrack closes that gap.

**EQ's Role**
EQ led the full product lifecycle: from qualitative user research (6 participants, interviews and focus groups) that produced the core "Aware Mover" persona, through CX design, product strategy, and engineering coordination with the Arcanys development team. The studio also designed the growth model — Strava integration as the primary acquisition channel, freemium conversion architecture, and an event-led marketing strategy (London Marathon 2026 partnership).

Built in-house: a proprietary hyperlocal air quality model (V2) incorporating LiDAR, building height data, open-source traffic and terrain layers — reducing dependency on third-party APIs.

**Disciplines applied:** User Research · Product Strategy · CX Design · Engineering (via Arcanys) · Growth & Marketing

**Key outcomes:**
- Multiple shipped versions (V2 through V4 in active development)
- Strava integration driving ~90% of user acquisition
- London Marathon 2026 event partnership
- Garmin integration in development — pushing AirTrack into the wearable ecosystem
- B2B API layer for clinical and enterprise use cases

**Persona insight:**
> *"For most users, there is no current tool for personal AQ monitoring. AirTrack is not replacing a competitor — it is creating a new behaviour."*
> — EQ Partners User Research, May 2025

### 7.5 Case Study: Interplay Survey

**Slug:** `/work/interplay`

**Overview**
Interplay is a B2B brand and sustainability assessment platform. It measures how effectively organisations connect their sustainability commitments to commercial and brand value — reframing sustainability as an opportunity, not a cost.

**The Problem**
Most sustainability reporting is compliance-led — it tells an organisation what they've done, not what it's worth. Brands and their agencies lack a rigorous, benchmarked tool for understanding the commercial potential of their sustainability position. Interplay fills that gap with a scored assessment and actionable financial model.

**EQ's Role**
EQ built the product from the ground up: defining the five-pillar scoring model (appetite for growth; scale & delivery capability; sustainability, brand, and business capability pillars), designing the digital survey and scoring engine, and creating the report output format — benchmarked brand reports with financial opportunity projections. A Digital Twin persona system — customer research transcripts turned into AI-powered avatars — enables clients to test product and messaging decisions against simulated customer reactions.

**Disciplines applied:** Product Strategy · Proposition Design · Survey & Scoring Architecture · Report Design · AI Integration

**Key outcomes:**
- Live scoring platform with multi-pillar assessment
- Automated brand benchmark reports
- Financial opportunity model integrated into output
- Digital Twin persona system for validation and insight

### 7.6 Case Study: Product OS

**Slug:** `/work/product-os`

**Overview**
Product OS is an AI-powered product intelligence infrastructure — a proprietary operating system for product teams that synthesises meeting notes, issue trackers, user research, and design principles into a live, queryable knowledge layer.

**The Problem**
Product teams lose enormous value between conversations. Decisions made in a meeting don't make it into the roadmap. Research insights don't travel to engineering. The signal is there — it just isn't connected. Product OS is the connective layer.

**EQ's Role**
EQ designed and built Product OS as internal infrastructure, initially deployed on AirTrack. It integrates with Granola (meeting notes), Linear (roadmap and issues), and user research archives. An agentic AI layer — built on Claude — surfaces roadmap intelligence, generates weekly reports, flags suggested issue changes, and maintains a living product knowledge base. It runs on a scheduled cadence, not just when asked.

**Disciplines applied:** Product Strategy · Systems Design · AI/Agentic Workflow Engineering · Product Operations

**Key outcomes:**
- Deployed on AirTrack as the operational intelligence layer
- Automated weekly roadmap reports and meeting intelligence
- AI persona simulation for product and strategy validation
- Proactive insight generation — the system flags issues, not just logs them

**EQ's POV:**
> *"Most product teams are drowning in information and starving for insight. Product OS is what happens when you build the connective tissue properly."*

### 7.7 Method

**Headline:** How we work

**Intro paragraph:**
EQ Partners works across the full product stack — not because we offer every service, but because we've learned that the problems in product development almost always live at the handoffs. Between strategy and design. Between design and engineering. Between launch and growth. We remove those handoffs.

**The six disciplines (each with a short paragraph):**

**Strategy**
We start with the problem, not the solution. Market context, user research, business model stress-testing, and competitive positioning. We don't move forward until we know what we're building and why it will win.

**CX Design**
Experience design grounded in real user behaviour. Personas, journeys, wireframes, and interface design — all developed from research, not assumption. We design for the person who will actually use it, not the person who commissioned it.

**Marketing**
Positioning, messaging, and acquisition strategy built in alongside the product — not bolted on after launch. We think about how people will discover and understand the product from day one.

**Sales**
For B2B products, we design the sales motion: the pitch, the proof points, the pipeline. We help partners get to first revenue, not just first launch.

**Engineering**
We coordinate and embed with development partners to ship real product. We work with lean, high-quality teams and manage the build to the same standard as the strategy and design.

**Customer Success**
Retention, feedback loops, and the product improvements that follow. We stay engaged after launch because that's when the most important learning happens.

---

**Our approach to AI**
We build AI into how we work, not just what we build. Our internal workflows — research synthesis, roadmap intelligence, persona simulation, product reporting — are agentic. This means we move faster, stay better-informed, and deliver more value per engagement than a traditional studio.

## 8. Design Direction

### Principles
- **Minimal and confident.** The work speaks. The site gets out of its way.
- **Type-led.** Strong typographic hierarchy. Avoid over-reliance on imagery or decoration.
- **Professional, not corporate.** This is a studio, not a management consultancy. Warmth within rigour.

### Visual References (mood)
- Linear.app, Vercel, Loom — product companies with clean, confident web presence
- Avoid: agency sites with heavy animation, full-bleed hero videos, or "award-winning" posturing

### Colour
- Primary: near-black (not pure #000 — softer, e.g. #0F0F0F or #111827)
- Background: off-white (#F9F9F7 or similar)
- Accent: a single warm or cool mid-tone — to be explored in design (suggest: a muted teal or warm amber — avoid generic "startup blue")
- Case study cards: each product should have a distinct accent colour for differentiation

### Typography
- Display/headline: A confident serif or clean geometric sans (e.g. Söhne, Canela, or similar premium options)
- Body: A legible, neutral sans (e.g. Inter, DM Sans)
- Avoid system fonts or anything that feels generic

### Imagery
- Product screens / UI details for case studies
- Abstract data/interface elements for hero sections
- No stock photography of people in meetings

### Motion
- Subtle: fade-ins, gentle scroll transitions
- No splash screens, heavy loaders, or parallax-for-its-own-sake
